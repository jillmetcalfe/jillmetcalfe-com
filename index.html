Two ways to start

1 hour

AI Foundations

Get past what’s stopping your team using AI, and give them a framework they can use the same afternoon.

What’s covered

  • How to think about AI: a very capable assistant, not magic and not a threat
  • Why using AI well is a communication skill, not a coding one
  • The RISEN™ framework: Role, Instruction, Steps, End goal, Narrowing
  • Live exercises, building prompts step by step

Key exercise

Together we build one prompt, adding a RISEN™ element at a time, and watch the output go from generic to genuinely professional.

“AI will be useful in my job, and I have a framework I can use straight away.”

Best for

  • Teams who need practical AI skills quickly
  • Organisations with limited time
  • Staff building confidence with the basics

3 hours

Complete AI Transformation

Everything in AI Foundations, plus a plan for where AI should and shouldn’t be used, how to use it safely, and a prompt library your team builds themselves.

What’s covered

  • The RISEN™ framework, including more advanced uses
  • Choosing which tasks to hand to AI, ranked by time saved against risk
  • Safety, privacy and company AI policy
  • Levels of AI use, from simple prompts to automation and agents
  • A long-term roadmap for adopting AI across the organisation

Key exercises

  • Task audit: map the team’s weekly work and sort it by time saved and risk
  • Micro-hackathon: small groups pick real tasks, write and test prompts, and present them. The results become your prompt library
  • Privacy and safety: hands-on with the settings and policies that matter for business use

“We have working prompts, a clear roadmap, agreed safety rules, and we know where to go next.”

Best for

  • Organisations ready to roll out AI properly
  • Leadership teams who need an implementation plan
  • Teams with time for deep, hands-on work

From €2,250 · 10 people × 3 hours

How it works

Mostly hands-on, built around your work

Learning by doing

Between half and two-thirds of every workshop is hands-on exercises. Using AI is a skill, and skills come from practice.

In person or online

In person works best, ideally with small tables so people can work together. Online sessions are available, with the exercises adapted.

Materials to keep

Everyone gets RISEN™ reference cards, prompt templates and a quick-start guide. The 3-hour workshop adds task-planning and risk templates, a tool comparison guide, a privacy and safety checklist, and templates for your prompt library and roadmap.

About

Hi, I’m Jill

For more than 30 years I worked as a professional translator and language specialist in international sport, NGOs and the watch industry. My job was making sure meaning survived the trip from one person to another, precisely.

That turns out to be exactly what using AI well comes down to. For the past three years I’ve worked as a Notion consultant, building knowledge systems and putting AI to work for small and medium-sized businesses in fintech, architecture, financial services and content creation. As a senior consultant with Matthias Frank Consulting, I run workshops regularly.
